# Commercial, Academic & Enterprise Services for FPembed

FPembed is an open-source cheminformatics toolkit for molecular representation and embedding workflows.

The project Maintainer provides engineering, custom algorithm work, and enterprise support directly.

## 1. Custom Collaboration Formats

Pick the format that fits your team, data, and timeline:

* **Hourly Expert Advisory (Time & Materials):** Technical consulting for computational chemistry teams, data engineering pipeline reviews, and performance troubleshooting.
* **Monthly Retainer:** Reserved engineering capacity for ongoing library maintenance, priority issue resolution, and algorithmic guidance.
* **Custom R&D & Descriptor Co-Development:** Engineering work to build, fine-tune, or accelerate proprietary molecular descriptors and embedding spaces for specific target classes, under strict NDA.
* **Joint Grant Proposals & Co-Authorship:** Academic and research partnerships for EU and national funding calls, including joint methodology development and algorithm integration.

## 2. Standard Service Packages & Institutional Frameworks

The Maintainer can structure a project as a fixed deliverable or route it through a regional procurement channel:

### A. Spain (Public Research & Local Industry)
* **Art. 60 LOSU / CSIC Framework:** Technical assistance and technology transfer contracts via university OTRI offices.
* **National Grants (AEI / CDTI):** External R&D subcontracting for national research projects.
* **Deliverables:** Local HPC deployment (RES Network), university lab onboarding, and biotech pipeline integration.

### B. European Union (Horizon Europe & EU Pharma)
* **Horizon Europe Subcontracting:** Dedicated work packages for EU funded research consortia (0% VAT via VIES / Reverse Charge).
* **EU Enterprise Support:** High-throughput screening scaling on EU cloud infrastructure (Databricks, AWS EU).
* **Deliverables:** Custom work packages, distributed compute optimization, and production integration.

### C. Global Enterprise (US, UK, Switzerland & Rest of World)
* **Enterprise Engineering:** C++ / CUDA bindings, private extension development, and memory optimization for multi-million compound libraries.
* **Guaranteed SLA:** Priority ticket resolution (24-48h response time), private communication channels (Slack/Teams), and custom licensing waivers if required.
